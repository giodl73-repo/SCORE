## Key findings this session

**CLUSTER D CONFIRMED (0012 — "Send in the Clowns"):** Dramatic-Functional Craft ratified. Three independent reviewers named it: Sondheim (from inside), Bernstein (from adjacent tradition), Pärt (from formal minimalism). Amendment language drafted and applied forward as v1.3. Innovations #25–26.

**CLUSTER E CONFIRMED (0013 — "Rhapsody in Blue"):** Synthesis Completeness ratified. Critical nuance: incomplete synthesis (one tradition load-bearing) = 11-13 Originality; complete synthesis (neither tradition primary) = 13-15. Gershwin and Bernstein both score as incomplete syntheses — neither classical architecture nor jazz character is wholly subsumed. Complete synthesis has not yet appeared in the corpus. Innovations #27–28.

**FIRST UNANIMOUS ORIGINALITY CEILING (0014 — Rite of Spring):** All five reviewers award 15/15. Ceiling Originality requires simultaneous rupture of multiple conventions, creating a space no incremental development could reach. Historical reception evidence (premiere riot) admitted as corroborating evidence, not cause. Innovation #29–30.

**FIRST NON-WESTERN WORK (0015 — Raga Yaman):** Three rubric modifications applied: Non-Western Craft vocabulary note, Modal-revelatory Step 0 sub-type, interpretive vs. compositional Originality distinction. Interpretive Originality at ceiling ≈ 11.25. Rubric held without rescoring. Innovations #31–32.

**REVISION CONFIRMED RUBRIC UTILITY (0016 — "The Long Way Back" v2):** Rubric-guided revision from 84.9 → 89.25 (+4.35). All predicted dimension movements confirmed. Originality unchanged — the revision was correctly a Craft and Structure achievement. Confirms the rubric as a compositional revision tool, not merely a retrospective scoring instrument.

## Top 3 priorities for next session

**1. Confirm Cluster F (Devotional Resonance)**

Cluster F needs one more confirming work where Resonance is organized around purpose (devotional, ritual, sacred function) rather than audience communication. Best candidate: **Bach "Erbarme Dich" (St. Matthew Passion BWV 244, No. 39)**. This work: (a) is devotional in the Western classical tradition, cross-checking whether purpose-as-resonance-category holds outside jazz; (b) activates Cluster D (dramatic-functional Craft — the alto aria serves a specific dramatic-devotional function in the Passion narrative); (c) allows Bach to review his own music (high tension value); (d) will provoke Beethoven as a hostile-but-engaged reviewer in devotional territory.

**2. Find the first Complete Synthesis (Cluster E extension)**

Cluster E confirmed that Gershwin and Bernstein are incomplete syntheses. No complete synthesis has yet appeared in the corpus — a work where neither source tradition recognizes itself as primary. Candidates: **Radiohead "Exit Music (For a Film)"** (art-song voice leading + rock production, neither dominant); or a recent work by a contemporary composer working deliberately across traditions. A complete synthesis would score 13-15 Originality and be the first work to reach the upper Cluster E tier.

**3. Exercise the Originality floor (8-11 range)**

The corpus has never scored a work below 11.25 on Originality. The 2-10 range of the anchors is unexercised. Two options: (a) score a period pastiche or commission written to conventional specification — confirms the lower anchors exist and function; (b) score a deliberate imitation (a Mozart Köchel work that is clearly minor and conventional) to test where the floor actually sits. Without floor calibration, the rubric cannot be fully trusted in the 8-12 Originality range.
