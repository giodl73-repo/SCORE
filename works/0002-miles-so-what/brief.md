## Structure

AABA modal form:

| Section | Key | Length |
|---------|-----|--------|
| A (head, call) | D Dorian | 8 bars |
| A (head, response) | D Dorian | 8 bars (total: 16) |
| B | Eb Dorian | 8 bars |
| A (return) | D Dorian | 8 bars |

Total head: 32 bars. Followed by improvised solos over the same AABA form.

**The "So What" motif:** The bass introduces a two-chord "call" (E–D over a chord pad) answered by the horns with "so what" (a two-chord response). This motif names the piece and establishes its anti-climactic, questioning stance.

## Tradition Context

"So What" is the opening track of *Kind of Blue* and the founding document of modal jazz as a mainstream practice. Before modal jazz, bebop and hard bop required soloists to navigate rapidly changing chord progressions (ii–V–I cycles, tritone substitutions, Coltrane changes). The modal approach replaces harmonic tension-and-release with tonal color: one mode for an extended duration, the soloist free to explore the mode's internal possibilities without the pressure of changes.

The philosophical shift is total: instead of "where are we going harmonically?" the question becomes "what can we find here?"
