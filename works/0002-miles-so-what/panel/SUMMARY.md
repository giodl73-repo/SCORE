## 3. Key Agreements

**Craft high across the panel (20-22 across all Craft-scoring reviewers).** Miles's muted trumpet placement, Evans's quartal voicings, Chambers's "so what" motif: recognized across traditions as precision. The one-point spread between Miles/Music Theorist (22) and Bach (20) tracks Bach's stricter view of contrapuntal discipline, not a disagreement about technical competence.

**Originality: consensus on the synthesis.** All three primary reviewers recognize that applying modal stasis to jazz -- replacing chord changes as the organizing logic -- was a genuinely original act. The elements were available; the synthesis was not predicted by them.

**Resonance: the piece achieves its intended effect across traditions.** Most remarkably, Beethoven (13) scores Resonance higher here than on the Crab Canon (11). Dwelling-as-arrival is an effect this piece achieves; formal-proof-as-feeling is not. The Listener (13) confirms from outside the tradition: "I joined it."

**Economy splits along the Structure axis.** Reviewers who score Structure highest score Economy highest; without developmental stopping signals, modal Economy discipline must be self-supplied by performers, and this is inconsistently achieved.

---

## 4. Key Tensions

**Tension 1: Modal stasis -- structure or absence?**

Miles: 22 ("the mode IS the architecture"). Music Theorist: 19 (modal form coherent on modal criteria). Bach: 15 ("two chords is not architecture"). Beethoven: 14 ("stasis refuses the arrow of time").

The 8-point spread is the highest dimension spread in any SCORE panel to date. The Music Theorist's score of 19 is the key datum: an analyst applying tradition-appropriate criteria finds the modal form coherent. Bach and Beethoven are applying developmental criteria to a non-developmental structure.

This tension generates Innovations #5 and #6 and confirms Cluster A.

**Tension 2: Economy when modal form removes the structural stopping signal.**

Developmental music has built-in Economy signals: recapitulation, cadence, resolution. Modal music does not. Miles (20): discipline largely maintained. Bach (15), Beethoven (16): inconsistently supplied. The Listener's 17 is the phenomenologically honest reading.

**Tension 3: Whether the B-section half-step shift earns its place.**

Miles: "it sounded right." Music Theorist: "coloristic, consistent with modal practice." Bach: "a transposition is not a structural event." No reviewer finds it harmful; the division is structural vs. decorative.

## 6. Cluster Analysis

**Cluster A CONFIRMED: Non-Developmental Architecture**

Innovation #1 (0001: Structural Self-Proof through palindromic form) and Innovations #5+6 (0002: modal stasis as architecture) point at the same rubric gap: the Structure anchors implicitly reward developmental form and cannot adequately assess structural strategies that achieve coherence through non-developmental mechanisms.

Two works. Three innovations. Same gap. **Cluster A is named and confirmed.**

**Cluster A trigger:** Proposed amendment to Structure anchors -- in non-developmental structures (modal, palindromic, and other forms), the formal mechanism is assessed for internal consistency and for the quality with which it creates the structural container the work requires, not against developmental criteria it was not designed to meet. Proposed for ratification as v1.1.
