Cluster E (Synthesis Originality) confirmed. Three independent namings:

Bernstein (#1): Synthesis-by-juxtaposition rather than synthesis-by-integration. Synthesis originality at high level, but not synthesis completeness. Named from position of a composer who worked in the same synthesis space.

Bach (#3): The classical structure is the organizing principle; the jazz materials are expressive content within that structure. The container is classical. Named from inside the classical tradition.

Critical nuance -- Incomplete Synthesis as a Category:
Synthesis can be original without being complete. Incomplete synthesis = 11-13 range. Complete synthesis = 13-15 range. Test: can you remove either tradition's primary structural contribution and still have the essential work?

Proposed Amendment Language (Cluster E -- for v1.3):

Complete synthesis (neither tradition recognizes itself as primary) occupies 13-15 range.
