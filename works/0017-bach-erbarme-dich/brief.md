## Structural Approach (Step 0)

**DEVELOPMENTAL** — Binary aria form (A-B-A da capo). The A section establishes the plea; the B section moves to relative major and secondary grief; the da capo return brings the opening with deeper weight. The form is conventional for a Baroque da capo aria, but the development happens within the structural frame through the interweaving of the vocal and violin lines. The form does not merely repeat — it accumulates.

## Context

Peter has just denied Christ three times. The cock has crowed. This aria is his response. The setting: alto voice (Peter's grief) and solo violin (the weeping that cannot be spoken). The two voices intertwine in an extended duet of lamentation. The harmonic language moves through chains of descending fifths. Duration: approximately 6-7 minutes.
