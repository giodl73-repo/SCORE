## Work Description

Raga Yaman is one of the most beloved ragas in the Hindustani classical tradition. It is an evening raga — traditionally performed in the early hours of evening, associated with longing, romantic feeling, and a particular quality of expansive beauty. It uses all seven notes of the swaras (equivalent to a scale) with the distinctive feature of a raised fourth (teevra Ma), giving it a Lydian character in Western terms.

The structure of a raga performance follows a developmental grammar:
- **Alap:** Unmetered, slow exploration of the raga's character. The performer introduces the raga's characteristic notes and phrases in the low register and slowly ascends. No rhythm instrument (or only a drone). The alap reveals the raga's emotional identity.
- **Jor:** The alap gains rhythmic regularity — a pulse emerges from the performer's own playing — but still without the tabla.
- **Jhala:** Rapid rhythmic development, still without tabla. The energy increases.
- **Gat:** The tabla enters. A composed melody (the gat) is stated, and improvisation begins within the rhythmic framework.

Raga Yaman's grammar is strict: certain characteristic phrases define the raga's identity; certain notes must be approached from specific directions; the raised fourth (Ga-Ma# in the ascent) is the raga's signature gesture.

## The Key Question

Does the SCORE rubric's Craft dimension apply to non-Western music? The Craft dimension's vocabulary (harmony, voice leading, voice leading) is Western. Raga Craft is about different technical mastery: gayaki (singing style), meend (glissando between notes), gamak (ornamental repetition), layakari (rhythmic play within the tala framework), and — most importantly — the raga's grammar: which notes precede which, which phrases define the raga's identity, what constitutes a deviation from the raga's established character.

Innovation #31 target: Non-Western Craft vocabulary — the technical mastery in raga performance is real and demanding but requires different Craft criteria.

Innovation #32 target: The alap as Structure — unmetered, improvised exploration of a modal space, organized by its own logic, is a form of structure that is simultaneously modal, improvisatory, and developmental.
