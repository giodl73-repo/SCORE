## 2. Gate Status

**Gate: PASS**

Aggregate score 85.5 is well above the advisory threshold (60). Within the expected range (84-90). The score reflects the significant panel spread on Structure (Beethoven at 13, Part at 22) while noting that Beethoven also reports unexpected Resonance (12 -- higher than expected from a reviewer who found the piece "almost incomprehensible as music"). Beethoven's Resonance score of 12 for a piece he cannot analyze using his own tradition's criteria is among the more remarkable findings of the founding sprint.

## 3. Key Agreements

**Craft of restraint recognized across traditions.** Part (23), Miles (22), Music Theorist (22): all recognize the negative craft required -- the piano player sustaining the arpeggio for ten minutes without ornamentation, the violinist sustaining tones without vibrato for thirty seconds, the structural choice to use two voices rather than four. Beethoven (18) docks most heavily for the absence of voice interaction (no counterpoint), but acknowledges the negative craft is real.

**Resonance: the piece surprises Beethoven.** This is the panel's most significant data point. Beethoven prepared for a piece that would leave him cold; he felt something. His Resonance score (12) is only slightly below Part's (14) and Miles's (14), and is above what his opening remarks predicted. The piece's resonance is real, documented by a reviewer constitutionally hostile to it.

**Originality of the tintinnabuli method: consensus.** Part (14), Miles (13), Beethoven (12): all recognize that the compositional method arrived at after eight years of silence is genuinely original. The only disagreement is over how much of the piece's originality consists of the method (high: all reviewers) vs. the specific deployment in this piece (lower: Miles notes "Fur Alina" established the method in the same form).

## 4. Key Tensions

**Tension 1: Structural stasis as spiritual argument vs. structural refusal.**

Part (22): "The method is the structure; the mirror logic is architecture; the sustained attention to the tonal center is the spiritual practice the piece enacts."

Miles (20): "The mirror logic is structure. I push on whether the extra eight minutes add to what the first two minutes established."

Beethoven (13): "There is no development. Elegance is not architecture. The arrow of time is refused and I do not know how to evaluate a music that refuses time."

Music Theorist (20): "The harmonic micro-structure (scale degrees' relationships to tonic triad) constitutes a valid structural system; the piece has structural content, though it operates on a much longer timescale than most traditions expect."

This is the panel's defining tension. It generates Innovation #10: structural stasis as spiritual argument. The tintinnabuli method is not the same stasis as modal jazz and not the same as palindromic structure -- it is a spiritual argument made through form, arguing that sustained attention to one thing reveals everything.

**Tension 2: Economy through subtraction -- what unit is the Economy measured in?**

Part (20): "Nothing wasted. The method generates exactly the notes required."

Miles (19): "I still want to know what the extra time added."

Beethoven (15): "Ten minutes is five times two minutes in terms of formal content."

Listener (18): "The descent covered emotional ground I had already arrived at."

The tension is not about whether the notes present are necessary (all agree they are, by the method's logic) but about whether the ten-minute duration is necessary. Part's answer: the duration is required by the mirror logic (ascent and descent). Beethoven's answer: the mirror logic doubles the content requirement without doubling the formal content. The Listener's answer: I had arrived by the ascent; the descent was the formal requirement, not the musical need.

This generates Innovation #9: Economy through subtraction. The current Economy anchors measure whether notes present are necessary. They do not address the question of whether the temporal container (the duration) is proportionate to the formal content. In a piece where the primary unit is a sustained tone held for thirty seconds, the Economy question is different from the Economy question in a piece where the primary unit is a note.

**Tension 3: The Resonance of stasis -- stillness-as-arrival vs. drama-as-arrival.**

Beethoven: "Resonance through surrender. Music that refuses drama can produce resonance of a different kind -- not triumph but stillness-as-arrival." He scores 12 despite his theoretical objections.

Part: "The piece creates the sonic conditions in which specific states can be inhabited. A listener who is open will inhabit them."

Miles: "Can you be present in a note? That is the question the piece is asking. The ones who can, receive what it has to give."

Listener: "I was in a place where nothing needed to happen next."

All reviewers converge on this: the piece produces a real resonance that is not the resonance of drama-and-resolution. The rubric's Resonance anchors are written for music that intends a felt effect (grief, joy, transcendence, tension, release). "Spiegel" intends a felt state that is not an effect but a condition: suspended time, the eternal present. This is a Resonance category the rubric does not yet fully address.

## 6. Cluster Analysis

**Structural Stasis taxonomy (three types, three works):**

The founding sprint has now identified three distinct uses of structural stasis:
- **Type 1 (0001):** Palindromic stasis -- the piece is its own proof through bidirectional identity
- **Type 2 (0002):** Modal stasis -- the mode creates a tonal gravity field that replaces harmonic development
- **Type 3 (0004):** Tintinnabuli stasis -- sustained attention to one harmonic field as spiritual practice

These are not the same phenomenon. The rubric's Structure anchors cannot assess them with the same language. A proposed Cluster B (Non-Developmental Architecture) from work 0002 now has a second instance; a third type of stasis is added in work 0004.

**Cluster B (Non-Developmental Architecture) now confirmed:** Works 0002 (modal stasis) and 0004 (tintinnabuli stasis) both point at the same Structure rubric gap: the anchors implicitly reward developmental form and cannot assess non-developmental structures on their own terms. The Cluster B amendment should be drafted:

*"Non-developmental structures are assessed against their own tradition's formal criteria, not against developmental criteria they were not designed to meet. For modal structures, assess the coherence and expressiveness of the tonal gravity field. For tintinnabuli and other constraint-method structures, assess the completeness with which the method generates the piece and the appropriateness of the material choices that precede the method's deployment."*

**Note on Cluster A relationship:** The tintinnabuli method is a possible Cluster A candidate (spiritual self-proof through form) but Part's review identifies a distinction from the Crab Canon and Fifth Symphony. "Spiegel" does not prove a claim about music; it enacts a spiritual practice through music. The practice and the piece are the same thing, but the proof is not of a musical claim but of a phenomenological one (sustained attention reveals everything). This is either an extension of Cluster A or a new cluster. Panel recommendation: hold this question until a second tintinnabuli work is reviewed.

## 7. Design Recommendations

**Recommendation 1: Ratify Cluster B amendment (Non-Developmental Architecture) alongside Cluster A (Structural Self-Proof).**
Both clusters are confirmed across two or more works. Both amendments are addition-only and forward-compatible.

**Recommendation 2: Economy annotation for constraint-method works.**
"In works where Economy is achieved through prior exclusion of pitch material (tintinnabuli, certain serial works, extreme minimalist works), Economy is assessed by whether the constraint produces a musical space commensurate with the work's duration and formal claims."

**Recommendation 3: Note Beethoven's Resonance finding.**
Beethoven (12) reported unexpected resonance from a piece he cannot analyze using his own tradition. This is the most significant data point in the panel: it confirms that "Spiegel im Spiegel" achieves its intended effect even for a listener constitutionally hostile to its formal premise. This is strong Resonance evidence. The Resonance score of 13.5 is likely conservative.

**Score note.** 85.5 is the correct v1.0 score. Under the proposed v1.1 amendments (Cluster A + Cluster B), the Structure score would likely rise from 19.1 to 22-23 (both modal and tintinnabuli criteria credited appropriately), bringing the aggregate to approximately 88-90.
