## Work 0011 Result and Identified Weakness

Work 0011 scored 84.9. The unanimous weakness identified by three reviewers (Miles, Sondheim, Ellington): **bridge bars 19-24 — the melodic climax was "melodically plain at the moment of maximum harmonic tension."**

Specifically: the climax arrives on the dominant of the mediant key (Gbmaj7 area) but the melody in bar 23 (the Bb7(#5) bar) simply moved stepwise rather than creating the leap or sustained note that the harmony demands. The peak was harmonically tense but melodically anonymous.

Three independent identifications:
- Miles: "the bridge's highest moment (bars 23-24, the Eb over the Bb7(#5)) is harmonically clear but melodically under-characterized"
- Sondheim: "the melodic peak must carry that weight through contour and harmony alone. The plain Eb at the peak is harmonically tense but melodically anonymous"
- Ellington: "the secondary-dominant chain repeated twice (bars 21-24) is the least personal in the composition"

---

## Revision Specification

Replace the stepwise melodic motion in bars 19-24 with a phrase that:

1. **Leaps up to the tension note (the major 7th of the new key center)** — from the Gb major area, the major 7th is F. A leap of a sixth or a seventh from the preceding note to F creates the contour the harmony demands.

2. **Sustains it for two beats before resolving stepwise downward** — the sustained F (major 7th) over the Bb7(#5) creates the specific tension-note relationship that the harmony has been building toward. The sustain allows the tension to register fully.

3. **Creates a second phrase that echoes the opening A section's chromatic descent motif — now in the bridge's mediant key** — the chromatic descent that was the inner-voice characteristic of bars 1-2 of the A section (Ebmaj7 → Eb7 → Abmaj7 → Abm7 → Db7) is now transposed and appears as a melodic gesture in the bridge's Gb major area. This connects the bridge melodically to the A section's distinctive idea, completing the bridge-as-necessity requirement (Innovation #23): the bridge now not only departs harmonically but brings the A section's distinctive idea to its melodic surface.
