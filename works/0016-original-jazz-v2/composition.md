## Overview

The revision addresses the one identified weakness in work 0011: the bridge's melodic peak (bars 19-24) was "melodically plain at the moment of maximum harmonic tension." The fix is targeted and surgical: only bars 19-24 are changed. All other elements of the composition are unchanged.

Form: 32-bar AABA, Eb major, medium swing at quarter note = 124 bpm.

## Bridge (Bars 17-24) — REVISED

The bridge departs to Gbmaj7 (the mediant — Gb is the flat-III of Eb major, reached via the raised-flat-side tendency established in the A section's Abm7-Db7).

**Bar 17:** Gbmaj7  
*Melody: Db (downward step from preceding Bb7 resolution) → Db (held)*  
*Entry into the bridge: Gb major established*

**Bar 18:** Gbm7 → Cb7  
*Melody: chromatic descent Cb → Bb → Ab*  
*This is the bridge's inner chromatic motion — echoing the A section's harmonic descent, now as inner melodic motion*

**Bar 19 (REVISED):** Fbmaj7  
*Melody: LEAP UP to F (the major 7th of Gb major) — interval of a major 6th upward from the preceding Ab*  
*The leap creates the contour the harmony demands. F is the tension note: it is the major 7th in the Gb major context, unresolved over the Fbmaj7 harmony.*  
*The leap is the revision. In version 1 (work 0011), this bar had stepwise motion. The leap recharacterizes the melodic peak.*

**Bar 20 (REVISED):** Ebm7 → Ab7  
*Melody: F sustained for 2 beats (THE REVISION — the sustained major 7th over the changing harmony)*  
*The sustain: F sustained over Ebm7-Ab7 creates a pedal-tone tension as the harmony moves beneath it. The F, which was the major 7th of Gb, is now the 9th of Ebm7 and the 6th of Ab7 — multiple simultaneous identities, all creating tension that demands resolution.*

**Bar 21 (REVISED):** Dbmaj7 → Db7  
*Melody: F resolves STEPWISE DOWNWARD — Eb → Db*  
*The stepwise downward resolution from the sustained F echoes the chromatic descent of the A section's inner voice. This is the revision's second element: the A section's distinctive idea (chromatic descent) now appears as a MELODIC gesture in the bridge, in the bridge's mediant key (Db/Gb area).*  
*This connects the bridge to the A section's distinctive idea at the melodic level — the bridge is now "about" the same thing the A section is about, transposed and made melodically explicit.*

**Bar 22:** Gm7 → C7  
*Melody: continues stepwise descent toward Eb return*  
*Bridge return preparation begins*

**Bar 23:** Fm7 → Bb7  
*Melody: approach phrase to the final A*

**Bar 24:** Bb7(#5)  
*Melody: Eb on the #5 chord — the half-step voice-leading pull (D# → Eb) prepares the tonic return*

## Analysis of the Revision

The revision makes three simultaneous improvements:

**1. Melodic peak now characterizes the moment.** The leap to F in bar 19 is not a generic ascending gesture — it is the specific leap to the tension note of the Gb major area, the note that most precisely identifies the harmonic position. The melody is no longer anonymous at the peak.

**2. The sustained tension note now serves the emotional arc.** The F held for two beats over the Ebm7-Ab7 change creates the emotional experience of "held at the edge of the bridge" — suspended between the bridge's departure and its return. The original version arrived at the peak and left immediately; the revision holds the peak long enough for the listener to experience it.

**3. The chromatic descent motif now completes the bridge-as-necessity test.** In version 1, the bridge migrated the distinctive idea from the harmonic level (inner voice) to the melodic level (bridge melody is a chromatic line). In version 2, the specific chromatic descent of the A section (the defining inner-voice gesture of bars 1-2) reappears in the bridge's resolution phrase (bar 21: F → Eb → Db), now in the bridge's mediant key. The bridge now demonstrates that the A section's distinctive idea works in the mediant key — it is not only an Eb major idea but a universal idea that the bridge reveals by transposing. This is a stronger bridge-as-necessity argument than version 1 provided.
