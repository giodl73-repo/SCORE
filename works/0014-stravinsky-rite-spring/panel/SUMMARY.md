## Panel Consensus and Divergences

UNANIMOUS ORIGINALITY AT CEILING: All five reviewers score Originality 15/15. This is the first unanimous ceiling score on any dimension in the corpus (Coltrane scored Resonance 15 alone in work 0009; this is the first time a full panel converges on a ceiling). The Originality ceiling is confirmed for this work.

Resonance divergence: Bach scores Resonance 11 vs. 13-14 from others. This reflects Bach's explicit acknowledgment that the primordial-violence expressive premise is outside his tradition's purposes for music. This is tradition-boundary limitation, not work failure -- the same pattern as Bach's Resonance outlier on "Somewhere" (0010) and Beethoven's on Spiegel im Spiegel (0004).

Miles highest (93): From a jazz perspective emphasizing Economy of means, the Rite's precision of rupture -- every element of the break serving maximum displacement with minimum excess -- scores highest on Economy (19) and Craft (23). Miles reads the irregular accents as an economy achievement: each accent is exactly where it needs to be to produce unease rather than energy.

## ORIGINALITY CEILING CONFIRMED: What It Means

All five reviewers score Originality 15/15. This is the first work in the SCORE corpus to receive a ceiling Originality score from the full panel. What does ceiling Originality mean?

The consensus: the work says something that could only be said this way, at this moment, by this composer, AND the saying requires a simultaneous rupture of multiple conventions that creates a new space outside the existing tradition. No incremental development could have reached this space. No prior work made it possible. No subsequent work can make it for the first time.

Beethoven's contribution: "The Rite's originality is historically uncopyable in the specific sense: no one can make this rupture again, because this rupture has already been made."

Bach's contribution: "I disagree with the premises but I recognize the originality." First instance in the SCORE corpus of a reviewer awarding ceiling Originality to a work they cannot fully appreciate on its own terms -- demonstrating that Originality is not tradition-specific even when Resonance is.

Historian's contribution: Historical evidence confirms rather than produces the ceiling score. The premiere riot, the paradigm shift, the century of response -- all confirm that the 1913-moment assessment of ceiling Originality was correct.

## Innovations from This Panel

#29 -- ORIGINALITY THROUGH SIMULTANEOUS RUPTURE

0014-stravinsky-rite-spring : Beethoven (primary: "breaking six conventions simultaneously creates a new space that no incremental development could have reached"), Miles (confirming from Economy angle: "each violation of convention earns its violation"), Music Theorist (analytical specification: "six conventions broken simultaneously"), Bach (naming from inside the broken tradition: "I cannot find the fugue here, but I recognize the departure from knowledge rather than departure from ignorance"), Historian (historical confirmation: the paradigm shift confirms the rupture was genuine) -- ORIGINALITY THROUGH SIMULTANEOUS RUPTURE -- Originality, Structure -- When a work breaks multiple established conventions simultaneously, it creates a new artistic space that no incremental development within the existing tradition could have reached; this is categorically different from extending a tradition (pushing one element to its limit while respecting others) or departing from a tradition in one respect; simultaneous rupture of multiple conventions creates a new space that is defined by the absence of the prior constraints; the canonical instance is the Rite of Spring (1913): polytonal harmony, irregular meter, extreme register, static repetition replacing development, and abandonment of the 19th-century expressive tradition -- all broken at once; the result is not an extreme version of the prior tradition but a genuinely new space; this justifies ceiling Originality scores (14-15) that cannot be assigned to works that extend their tradition however brilliantly.

#30 -- HISTORICAL RUPTURE AS ORIGINALITY EVIDENCE

0014-stravinsky-rite-spring : Historian (primary: "the premiere riot documents a gap between the work's originality and the audience's preparedness; the paradigm shift that followed confirms ceiling Originality"), Beethoven (confirming from inside revolutionary experience: "I know what it feels like to make music the audience is not ready to hear"), Bach (confirming from the reception side: the work "is neither disorder nor my tradition -- it is something else") -- HISTORICAL RUPTURE AS ORIGINALITY EVIDENCE -- Originality -- When a work causes a paradigm shift -- when subsequent composers must either embrace, reject, or find an alternative to its structural logic -- this is evidence of ceiling Originality at the moment of creation; the rubric scores Originality at the moment of creation, not in retrospect; historical rupture evidence confirms rather than produces the ceiling score; the test: did subsequent composers have to take a position on this work? If yes, the work was genuinely new; if no, it was an excellent contribution to an existing tradition without paradigm-shifting originality; the premiere riot specifically: not evidence of failure but of audience-calibration gap -- the audience was not ready for something genuinely new; the subsequent universal reception confirms the work's Originality was real, not merely shocking.
