# SCORE Innovations Log

Append-only. Format: `WORK:REVIEWER — TECHNIQUE NAME — SCORE DIMENSION — IMPLICATION`

Innovations are logged after every panel session. A panel that identifies no innovations has not looked hard enough. The rubric is not complete; it is the current best description of what good music does. Every work reveals something the rubric has not yet named.

When 2+ innovations point at the same rubric gap across 2+ works, a cluster is named. Clusters trigger proposed amendments. Amendments are forward-only.
