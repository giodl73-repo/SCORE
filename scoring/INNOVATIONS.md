# SCORE Innovations Log

Append-only. Format: `WORK:REVIEWER -- TECHNIQUE NAME -- SCORE DIMENSION -- IMPLICATION`

Innovations are logged after every panel session. A panel that identifies no innovations has not looked hard enough. The rubric is not complete; it is the current best description of what good music does. Every work reveals something the rubric has not yet named.

When 2+ innovations point at the same rubric gap across 2+ works, a cluster is named. Clusters trigger proposed amendments. Amendments are forward-only.

## Panel: 0001-bach-crab-canon (2026-04-25)

**#1** `0001-bach-crab-canon : Bach, Beethoven, Miles (convergent) -- STRUCTURAL SELF-PROOF -- Originality, Structure -- The work whose formal structure constitutes a logical proof of its own claim about music; the piece IS the argument and cannot be paraphrased without losing what it demonstrates. Music equivalent of PROSE Cluster A (self-demonstrating text). Named from three angles: Bach ("the piece proves by existing"), Beethoven ("the mathematics are the argument"), Miles ("you were looking for the thing that doesn't change when time reverses"). Candidate seed for SCORE Cluster A.`

**#2** `0001-bach-crab-canon : Bach, Beethoven, Miles (convergent) -- BIDIRECTIONAL ECONOMY -- Economy -- Economy that is structurally guaranteed by the formal rule rather than achieved through compositional discipline; in a palindromic canon, every note serves both the forward and retrograde reading simultaneously, making waste logically impossible. Raises the question: does Economy score the choice of the form, or the discipline within the form? Candidate for rubric annotation.`

**#3** `0001-bach-crab-canon : Beethoven (primary), Bach and Miles (partial) -- RESONANCE-PROOF GAP -- Resonance -- The gap between formal proof and felt experience: a piece can constitute a logical proof about music without producing that proof as felt experience in the listener. Admiration and feeling are not the same response. For a listener who understands the palindrome, there may be awe; for a listener who does not, there may be only pleasure at the counterpoint. The intended effect (felt suspension in bidirectional time) and the achieved effect (admiration for formal elegance) diverge for some listeners.`

**#4** `0001-bach-crab-canon : Historian (primary) -- ECONOMY FOR ONE -- Economy -- Economy calibrated to a single expert listener rather than a general audience; the absence of explanatory material, transition passages, or recapitulation that would help a non-expert catch up, treated as an asset rather than a deficit when the intended audience is one sophisticated listener. The Crab Canon was composed for Frederick the Great -- one person -- and its economy is calibrated entirely to that interlocutor. Audience calibration of Economy not currently captured by the rubric.`

## Panel: 0002-miles-so-what (2026-04-26)

**#5** `0002-miles-so-what : Miles (primary), Beethoven (naming the gap) -- STRUCTURAL STASIS AS FORMAL ARGUMENT -- Structure -- When a piece refuses harmonic development, the refusal is itself a structural decision; modal stasis is not the absence of structure but an alternative structural principle organized through tonal gravity rather than harmonic progression; D Dorian is not "less structure than sonata form" but "a different kind of structure with its own excellence criteria." The Structure anchors reward developmental form and cannot distinguish between "no structure" and "modal structure." Seed of Cluster B (Non-Developmental Architecture), confirmed at work 0004.`

**#6** `0002-miles-so-what : Music Theorist (primary), Miles (convergent) -- THE MODE AS STRUCTURE -- Structure -- In modal music, the tonal center creates the structural container that harmonic development creates in tonal music; the gravitational field of the modal center establishes home, defines tension and release, and gives the improviser somewhere to be and somewhere to return to; modal architecture should be scored against modal criteria (coherence of the modal center, quality of the establishing gesture, coherence of improvisers' relationship to the tonal field) rather than against developmental criteria it was not designed to meet. Cluster B confirmed alongside #5.`

## Panel: 0003-beethoven-fifth (2026-04-26)

**#7** `0003-beethoven-fifth : Beethoven (primary), Bach, Bernstein, Music Theorist (convergent from different angles) -- STRUCTURAL SELF-PROOF IN DEVELOPMENTAL FORM -- Originality, Structure -- The Fifth Symphony's first movement proves "four notes contain a symphony" by developing four notes into a symphony; the proof is the structure; to understand the claim you must hear the movement; the work cannot be paraphrased without losing what it demonstrates; this is the same structural self-proof category as the Crab Canon (Innovation #1) but achieved through developmental rather than palindromic means. CLUSTER A CONFIRMED: two works, two mechanisms (palindrome, development), one category -- works whose form constitutes their argument. The cluster covers both non-developmental (Crab Canon) and developmental (Fifth Symphony) structures; the distinguishing criterion is self-proof, not developmental vs. non-developmental.`

**#8** `0003-beethoven-fifth : Beethoven (primary), Bach, Bernstein (convergent via coda-length debate) -- ECONOMY IN DEVELOPMENTAL MUSIC -- THE EXPANSION OF NECESSITY -- Economy -- The coda of the Fifth Symphony is "too long" by conventional Economy standards, but Economy in developmental music must be measured against the material's requirements, not against conventional expected length; the correct Economy test is: "Did the development end when the material was exhausted?" -- not "Did the development end when the listener expected?"; a coda that continues because the material requires it is economical; a coda that continues because the composer did not know how to end is not. The Economy anchors do not currently distinguish between Economy in non-developmental music (is each note necessary?) and Economy in developmental music (did the development continue as long as the material required?).`

## Panel: 0004-part-spiegel (2026-04-26)

**#9** `0004-part-spiegel : Part (primary), Listener (phenomenological confirmation), Miles (probing the duration question) -- ECONOMY THROUGH SUBTRACTION -- Economy -- The most economical choice in a tintinnabuli work is to use almost nothing; the Economy dimension measures whether notes present are necessary, but does not address pieces where the primary formal unit is a sustained tone held for many beats, or where the economic act is the prior decision to exclude almost all pitch material; Economy through subtraction is achieved by constraint -- the constraint produces the economy -- and requires a different assessment frame: "does the constraint produce a musical space commensurate with the work's duration and formal claims?" Cluster B second instance confirmed (alongside Innovation #5, work 0002).`

**#10** `0004-part-spiegel : Part (primary), Beethoven (naming the gap from hostility), Listener (felt-experience confirmation) -- STRUCTURAL STASIS AS SPIRITUAL ARGUMENT -- Structure, Resonance -- Part's tintinnabuli stasis is not the refusal of development (modal jazz, Innovation #5) nor the mathematical proof through palindrome (Crab Canon), but a spiritual argument that sustained attention to one thing reveals everything; the stasis is the practice, not the container for the practice; the piece IS what it demonstrates; three distinct structural stasis types now identified in the founding sprint: (1) palindromic proof (0001), (2) modal tonal gravity (0002), (3) tintinnabuli spiritual argument (0004). The rubric needs language for each type, and the Resonance dimension needs to address resonance-through-surrender (Beethoven's finding) alongside resonance-through-drama.`

## Panel: 0005-parker-koko (2026-04-26)

**#11** `0005-parker-koko : Coltrane (primary), Miles, Performer, Music Theorist (convergent definition); Beethoven (naming the gap from inability to apply alternative) -- IMPROVISED CRAFT VS. COMPOSED CRAFT -- Craft -- The rubric's Craft dimension asks whether technical choices are "precisely made," which requires fundamentally different criteria for improvised vs. composed music; improvised Craft is assessed on: (a) motor execution at tempo -- is the physical execution of the improvised line precisely controlled at the required tempo? (b) harmonic navigation without deliberation -- does the improviser correctly navigate the harmonic terrain at performance tempo, including unexpected changes and errors by other performers? (c) melodic logic under pressure -- does the improvised line maintain musical coherence and arc across the duration of the improvisation, despite having been generated in real time? These are not inferior to composed-craft criteria but different; a separate Craft mode is required. THE MOST IMPORTANT CLUSTER CANDIDATE OF THE FOUNDING SPRINT. Seed of Cluster C (Improvised Music).`

**#12** `0005-parker-koko : Coltrane (primary), Miles, Performer, Music Theorist (convergent); Beethoven (naming the gap from applied-wrong-criteria position) -- REAL-TIME ARCHITECTURE -- Structure -- Parker's solo has structure (building, developing, resolving) that was not written in advance but emerged in performance; the Structure dimension needs to handle architecture that was found while being made, not planned before execution; real-time architecture is not inferior to pre-planned architecture but requires different assessment: the capacity to find a complete arc in real time is itself a structural achievement; the quality of the arc found should be assessed on the same standards as pre-planned architecture; the process by which it was achieved (not planned) is noted but does not disqualify it as architecture. Cluster C second element, alongside Innovation #11.`

## Cluster Status (after founding sprint, works 0001-0005)

**Cluster A: Structural Self-Proof** -- CONFIRMED (works 0001, 0003)
Works whose formal structure constitutes a logical proof of their own claim about music. The proof is not made by the work; the proof IS the work. Works in this cluster cannot be paraphrased without losing what they demonstrate. Instances: palindromic proof (0001-Crab Canon), developmental proof (0003-Fifth Symphony).
Amendment status: PROPOSED for v1.1 ratification.

**Cluster B: Non-Developmental Architecture** -- CONFIRMED (works 0002, 0004)
Works whose structural coherence does not derive from harmonic development and resolution but from an alternative mechanism (modal tonal gravity, tintinnabuli method, or similar). The Structure anchors implicitly reward developmental form; these works require assessment against their own tradition's formal criteria.
Amendment status: PROPOSED for v1.1 ratification alongside Cluster A.

**Cluster C: Improvised Music** -- SEEDED (work 0005)
Works in which the primary creative act is real-time improvisation rather than pre-planned composition. Requires improvisation mode in Craft dimension and extension of Structure anchors for real-time architecture. Needs one additional work to confirm.
Amendment status: PROPOSED; held pending Cluster C confirmation.
