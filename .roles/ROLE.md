# SCORE Role Index

SCORE is a music design lab. Use these roles when changing the rubric, composer
panels, reviews, original works, or public explanation of the system.

## Parliament

| File | Role | Primary tension |
|---|---|---|
| `parliament/rubric-steward.md` | Rubric Steward | Evolving the rubric vs. preserving score comparability |

## Craft

| File | Role | Primary tension |
|---|---|---|
| `craft/composition-craft-auditor.md` | Composition Craft Auditor | Original effect vs. musical construction |

## Audience

| File | Role | Primary tension |
|---|---|---|
| `audience/listener-response-reviewer.md` | Listener Response Reviewer | Composer intent vs. listener experience |

## Review order

1. Use Composition Craft Auditor for structure, harmony, rhythm, texture, or form.
2. Use Listener Response Reviewer when a piece claims a listener effect.
3. Use Rubric Steward before changing dimensions, weights, or scoring protocol.

## PITFALL gate routing

Invoke the Rubric Steward before works 0017-0018, Cluster F, innovation IDs
#33-34, handoff docs, README status, or future v1.4 language is treated as
ledger-reconciled, complete, safe for ID reuse, or ratified rubric amendment
history.

Invoke Rubric Steward, Composition Craft Auditor, and Listener Response Reviewer
before persona panels, weighted SCORE results, or role reviews are used as
definitive musicological, historical, licensing, canonical, final music, or
source-rights authority.

Invoke Composition Craft Auditor, Listener Response Reviewer, and Rubric
Steward before a panel `SUMMARY.md`, weighted score, PASS label, innovation
cluster, tradition note, or handoff is treated as permission to revise a
composition, ratify an amendment, rescore older works, publish a score,
redistribute source material, or skip dissent and workflow selection.
