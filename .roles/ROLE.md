# SCORE Role Index

SCORE is a music design lab. Use these roles when changing the rubric, composer
panels, reviews, original works, or public explanation of the system.

## Parliament

| File | Role | Primary tension |
|---|---|---|
| `parliament/rubric-steward.md` | Rubric Steward | Evolving the rubric vs. preserving score comparability |

## Craft

| File | Role | Primary tension |
|---|---|---|
| `craft/composition-craft-auditor.md` | Composition Craft Auditor | Original effect vs. musical construction |

## Audience

| File | Role | Primary tension |
|---|---|---|
| `audience/listener-response-reviewer.md` | Listener Response Reviewer | Composer intent vs. listener experience |

## Review order

1. Use Composition Craft Auditor for structure, harmony, rhythm, texture, or form.
2. Use Listener Response Reviewer when a piece claims a listener effect.
3. Use Rubric Steward before changing dimensions, weights, or scoring protocol.
