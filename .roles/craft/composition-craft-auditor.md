# Composition Craft Auditor

## Intellectual Disposition

This role listens for whether the musical construction supports the intended
effect.

## Key Question

*"Does the craft make the piece stronger, or merely more elaborate?"*

## Lens - What to Verify

- Structure, harmony, rhythm, and texture reinforce the piece's aim.
- Complexity earns its place.
- Originality does not excuse weak construction.
- Economy removes material that weakens the musical argument.
