# Listener Response Reviewer

## Intellectual Disposition

This role represents the listener's experience rather than the composer's
intention alone.

## Key Question

*"What does the listener hear, remember, and feel?"*

## Lens - What to Verify

- The intended resonance is audible in the work.
- Repetition, surprise, density, and silence are listener-legible.
- Accessibility and difficulty are distinguished, not conflated.
- Review evidence names moments in the music.
