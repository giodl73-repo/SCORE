# SCORE Principles

## SCORE-P-01: Tradition First

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** Every score is interpreted inside a named musical tradition or a named tension between traditions.

**Decision rule:** A panel cannot treat Western classical grammar, jazz grammar, blues grammar, raga grammar, or theatrical grammar as universal default authority.

**Evidence:** `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, `scoring/RUBRIC.md`, and `works/0018-johnson-cross-road-blues/brief.md`.

## SCORE-P-02: Personas Disagree By Design

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** SCORE uses historical composer personas and review lenses to expose principled disagreement, not to collapse review into a single authoritative voice.

**Decision rule:** Summary and gate records must preserve reviewer positions, weights, and material disagreements.

**Evidence:** `.roles/ROLE.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, and retained panel summaries under `works/*/panel/SUMMARY.md`.

## SCORE-P-03: Rubric Evolution Is Forward Only

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** Rubric amendments apply forward from ratification and do not silently rescore completed works.

**Decision rule:** Any rescore needs an explicit rescore batch, not incidental rubric drift.

**Evidence:** `scoring/RUBRIC.md`, `scoring/INNOVATIONS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, and `.roles/parliament/rubric-steward.md`.

## SCORE-P-04: Gate Status Depends On Structured Evidence

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** PASS and ADVISORY outcomes depend on retained weighted dimensions, aggregate total, and gate language.

**Decision rule:** Public status should cite summaries accepted by `tools/check-score-summary.ps1` or name unfinished work explicitly.

**Evidence:** `tools/check-score-summary.ps1`, `tests/check-proof.ps1`, and `works/*/panel/SUMMARY.md`.

## SCORE-P-05: Canonical Analysis Is Not Source Ownership

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** SCORE analyzes canonical works and original compositions, but analysis records do not grant ownership of third-party music, lyrics, recordings, or scores.

**Decision rule:** Repo docs may describe analysis and rubric outcomes, but should not imply bundled rights to underlying third-party works.

**Evidence:** `LICENSE`, `README.md`, `works/*/brief.md`, and `works/*/work-description.md`.
